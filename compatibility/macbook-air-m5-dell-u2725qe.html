Exact-model compatibility

MacBook Air M5 → Dell U2725QE

Verdict · Manufacturer-confirmed

Compatible

The direct path is Thunderbolt 4 (USB-C) → Thunderbolt 4 upstream. The MacBook Air M5 supports DisplayPort over Thunderbolt 4 and up to 4K at 240 Hz for one external display. Dell specifies the U2725QE at 3840×2160 up to 120 Hz with a Thunderbolt 4 upstream input and up to 140 W Power Delivery.

What you need

Use a Thunderbolt 4 cable capable of carrying display data and power. For this pairing, a separate video adapter is not inherently required.

Target: 3840×2160 up to 120 Hz.
Charging: The display can provide more power than the MacBook Air requires, with USB Power Delivery negotiating the supported level.
One cable: Yes — video, USB data and charging can share the Thunderbolt connection.

Why ELORUMTECH says yes

Apple documents two Thunderbolt 4 USB-C ports with DisplayPort 1.4/HBR3 and DSC capability on MacBook Air M5. Dell documents Thunderbolt 4 upstream with DisplayPort 1.4/DSC, 140 W EPR Power Delivery, and a maximum 4K/120 Hz panel mode on the U2725QE. The source and destination therefore share a direct Thunderbolt/DisplayPort path.

Keep yours

If you already have a compliant Thunderbolt 4 cable, ELORUMTECH does not identify a reason to buy an adapter or dock just to connect these two devices.

Confidence: Manufacturer-confirmed. Exact refresh-rate availability can still depend on macOS settings, cable quality and the rest of the active display configuration.
