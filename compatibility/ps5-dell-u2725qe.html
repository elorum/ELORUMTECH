Exact-model compatibility

PlayStation 5 → Dell U2725QE

Verdict · Manufacturer-confirmed

Compatible for 4K up to 120 Hz

Use the PS5 HDMI output directly into the U2725QE HDMI input. PlayStation documents HDMI 2.1 and 4K/120 Hz output. Dell specifies its HDMI input for UHD 3840×2160 at 120 Hz with HDMI 2.1 FRL and VRR.

Best connection path

PS5 HDMI OUT → Ultra High Speed HDMI cable → Dell U2725QE HDMI IN.

Use the cable supplied with the PS5 or another certified Ultra High Speed HDMI cable. A USB-C or Thunderbolt adapter is unnecessary for the direct console-to-monitor path.

120 Hz and VRR

120 Hz must be enabled in PS5 video-output settings and the game must support a 120 Hz mode. PlayStation also supports VRR on compatible HDMI 2.1 displays; Dell lists VRR on this monitor's HDMI 2.1 input.

Keep yours

If you still have the HDMI cable supplied with the PS5 and it is working correctly, keep it. The connection itself does not justify buying another cable.

Confidence: Manufacturer-confirmed for the hardware path. Individual games determine whether a 120 fps mode is available.
