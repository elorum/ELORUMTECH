Analytics

Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-NBJBV3XERH) is off until you switch it on. On your first visit a banner asks for your analytics choice; no measurement script is requested from Google before you answer.

  • Choosing Allow analytics saves the value granted in localStorage (key elorumtech-analytics-consent). The Google tag then loads with anonymize_ip: true, and from that point Google Analytics sets its cookies in your browser.
  • Choosing Necessary only saves denied under the same key, and the Google tag never loads.

Your saved answer is reused on later visits, so the banner does not reappear. To change it, clear this site’s stored data in your browser and the banner will ask again.
