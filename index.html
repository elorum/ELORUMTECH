Black screen / “no signal”

Check the cheap things first: the display input selected, a different port, a video-capable cable (charge-only USB-C cables carry no picture), and firmware. Then follow the symptom checklist in the USB-C → HDMI guide.

Stuck at 60 Hz

Every hop must support the mode: host port, adapter or dock, cable and the display input. Hubs and many adapters cap refresh. See USB-C laptop → 4K monitor: stuck at 60 Hz.

PS5 won’t do 4K 120 Hz

Sony’s checklist starts with the TV’s HDMI 2.1 port, TV firmware, the included or Ultra High Speed cable, and removing a receiver from the path. See PS5 4K 120 Hz troubleshooting.

Why connector shape is not enough

USB-C and HDMI connections can look identical while supporting different video, data, charging and refresh-rate capabilities. A useful compatibility check must consider the source device, protocol, cable or adapter, destination and the result you want.

Quick FAQ

What does ELORUMTECH check before recommending a cable or dock?

The full path: source capability, protocol, cable/adapter/dock, destination, and the outcome you want—then whether to keep what you already have.

Why is USB-C alone not enough?

USB-C is a connector shape. Ports differ in Alt Mode video, USB4, Thunderbolt, data speed and Power Delivery.

When does ELORUMTECH say keep what you have?

When your existing path already meets resolution, refresh, charging and feature requirements.
