Why connector shape is not enough

USB-C and HDMI connections can look identical while supporting different video, data, charging and refresh-rate capabilities. A useful compatibility check must consider the source device, protocol, cable or adapter, destination and the result you want.

Laptop to monitor

Check video over USB-C, monitor input capability, charging and whether one cable can do the whole job.

Laptop to dock

Distinguish USB-C, USB4 and Thunderbolt requirements before selecting a dock or host cable.

Console to display

Match HDMI cable class and display capability to goals such as 4K and 120 Hz.
