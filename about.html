About ELORUMTECH

Updated: 2026-09-19

ELORUMTECH helps people make devices work together—laptops, phones, tablets, handhelds and consoles to monitors, docks and chargers—by checking the full connection path before anyone buys another cable.

What we do

We run a Compatibility Engine: you say what you have, what you want to connect it to, and what you want it to do (video, refresh, charging, data, one-cable, optional capture). We return a clear outcome category, path text, advice, and keep-yours guidance when existing hardware is enough.

Guides and exact-model pages explain standards-backed patterns (USB-C Alt Mode, HDMI bandwidth classes, Thunderbolt one-cable, USB Power Delivery). We use confidence language and ask you to verify exact models.

What we do not do

We do not invent exact-model Hz claims. We do not force capture hardware onto play-only console→TV paths. We do not let product or affiliate incentives decide the technical answer.
