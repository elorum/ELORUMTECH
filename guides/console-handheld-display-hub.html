Console & handheld display hub

No signal, no 120 Hz, wrong resolution, or a receiver or dock that breaks the picture. Pick your device and symptom: you get the maker's own steps in a safe order, each with its source and the date we checked it. Where the maker publishes nothing, we say UNKNOWN instead of guessing.

Updated: 2026-10-18 · Sources checked: 2026-10-18

By Nicki A.

Short answer: connect the console directly to the screen's HDMI input first and remove receivers, splitters and adapters. If the screen stays black, use the maker's low-resolution recovery (table below) instead of resetting the console. For 120 Hz, the screen must accept 120 Hz over HDMI; a monitor's DisplayPort refresh rate does not count for an HDMI path.
Confidence: OEM support pages (Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo, Valve, ASUS, Lenovo) + HDMI Licensing Administrator • checked 2026-10-18 • UNKNOWN where no OEM statement

Common wrong advice, corrected by the makers

Wrong: "Xbox can't do 120 Hz at all without HDMI 2.1."
Right: Xbox Support: on Series S or a TV without HDMI 2.1 you can still set 120 Hz at a lower resolution. If a mode both devices support is missing, Video fidelity & overscan → Overrides → HDMI lets you pick it (it reverts after 20 s if the TV rejects it). Source: Xbox Support, checked 2026-10-18.

Wrong: "My monitor is 165 Hz, so the console will get 120 Hz."
Right: Microsoft: some monitors support 120 Hz over DisplayPort but not over HDMI, and Xbox outputs HDMI. Check the HDMI input limits. Source: Xbox Support, checked 2026-10-18.

Wrong: "Any USB-C charger or the old Switch adapter works on the Switch 2 dock."
Right: Nintendo: HAC-002 cannot power the dock. Use NGN-01 or an adapter and cable with at least 54W. Source: Nintendo Support, checked 2026-10-18.

Wrong: "A cheap DisplayPort-to-HDMI adapter is fine for the Steam Deck dock."
Right: Valve: passive DP-to-HDMI adapters are not supported. Use an active adapter. Source: Steam Support, checked 2026-10-18.

Wrong: "If the box says HDMI 2.1, the cable is fine."
Right: HDMI Licensing Administrator: look for the Ultra High Speed HDMI Cable Certification Label (scannable QR) and the cable name on the jacket. Resellers may use other marketing terms. Source: HDMI LA, checked 2026-10-18.

Wrong: "Putting the receiver in the chain can't hurt."
Right: PlayStation lists a receiver or other intermediary equipment as a 4K 120 Hz cause. Microsoft says VRR may cause display issues through an AVR and suggests bypassing it. Source: PlayStation · Xbox Support, checked 2026-10-18.

Wrong: "A factory reset is the first step for a black screen."
Right: It is not the first step. Start with the display-only recovery above. Microsoft and PlayStation both offer a low-resolution or Change Video Output route first; PlayStation warns that some Safe Mode options delete data. Source: PlayStation Safe Mode · Xbox Support, checked 2026-10-18.

FAQ

How do I get a picture back when my console shows no signal?

Use the maker's recovery route. Xbox Series X: hold Power + Eject until the second beep to start at 640 x 480. Xbox Series S: hold the Xbox button + Pair. PS5: start Safe Mode and choose Change Video Output. Steam Deck: turn on External display safe mode in the Display menu before docking. For Switch 2, Nintendo's no-video page starts with the dock power adapter (NGN-01, or at least 54W) and an Ultra High Speed HDMI cable; we found no documented low-resolution mode.

Can Xbox run 120 Hz without HDMI 2.1?

Yes, at a lower resolution. Microsoft's 'Set up 4K gaming at 120 Hz' article says that on a Series S, or a TV without HDMI 2.1, you can set Refresh rate to 120 Hz and the console lowers the resolution. The display must support 120 Hz over HDMI. For 4K at 120 Hz, Microsoft's articles disagree: 'Set up 4K gaming at 120 Hz' lists an Xbox Series X, an Ultra High Speed HDMI cable that supports HDMI 2.1 and an HDMI 2.1 TV, and says Series S owners who wish to game in 4K@120Hz need a certified Ultra High Speed HDMI cable; 'How to change the TV display settings' says 120Hz is only available for 1080p and 1440p.

My monitor is 144 Hz. Why does my console not offer 120 Hz?

Xbox sends video over HDMI, and Microsoft states that some monitors support 120 Hz over DisplayPort but not over HDMI. Check the monitor's HDMI input limits, not the DisplayPort headline number.

Can I use my old Nintendo Switch adapter with the Switch 2 dock?

No. Nintendo says the original Switch AC adapter (HAC-002) can power the Switch 2 console directly but cannot provide enough power for the dock. Use the Switch 2 adapter (NGN-01) or another adapter and cable with at least 54W.

Does any DisplayPort-to-HDMI adapter work with the Steam Deck dock?

No. Valve says passive DisplayPort-to-HDMI adapters are not supported and may cause issues. Use an active cable or adapter.

What is the maximum external refresh rate of the ROG Xbox Ally or Legion Go?

UNKNOWN. The ASUS and Lenovo spec pages we checked list the USB-C ports and DisplayPort support but no maximum external resolution or refresh rate. We do not guess it.
