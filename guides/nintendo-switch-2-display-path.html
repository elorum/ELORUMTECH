Nintendo Switch 2 → display path

TV Mode video goes through the Nintendo Switch 2 dock HDMI path. Keep the official dock, Ultra High Speed HDMI, and Switch 2 AC adapter. Do not treat Switch 2 like a Steam Deck Alt Mode host.

Updated: 2026-09-19

Short answer (KEEP / dock-required): Switch 2 console → Nintendo Switch 2 dock → Ultra High Speed HDMI → TV/monitor HDMI IN, with the Switch 2 AC adapter (NGN-01 class) powering the dock. Play-only does not require capture. Skip generic USB-C→HDMI “without dock” assumptions.
Confidence: Nintendo primary docs • third-party docks = verify maker (no matrix here)

1. KEEP the official dock path

Documented TV Mode stack

Nintendo Support’s “How to connect to a TV” flow requires:

  • Nintendo Switch 2 console
  • Nintendo Switch 2 dock
  • Nintendo Switch 2 AC Adapter (model no. NGN-01)
  • Ultra High Speed HDMI cable (included with Switch 2; original Switch HDMI is not Ultra High Speed and cannot be used for Switch 2 TV mode)

Tech Specs list video output via the HDMI connector in TV mode (up to 4K60 class; 120 fps at 1080p/1440p when selected)—not as a laptop-style USB-C monitor upstream.

Wrong paths to avoid

  • USB-C Alt Mode direct to a USB-C/portable monitor — not the documented TV Mode path; do not claim success from connector shape alone.
  • Original Switch dock / HAC AC / non–Ultra High Speed HDMI — Nintendo documents these as unsuitable for Switch 2 TV mode.
  • Laptop Thunderbolt/USB-C docks — wrong category for Switch 2 living-room video.

2. Play-only → skip capture

Viewing ≠ recording

If you only need the TV or monitor to show the game, stop at dock → HDMI → display. Capture cards belong only when you intentionally record or stream—see the capture guide for contrast, not as a play requirement.

3. Cable class only when needed

Ultra High Speed HDMI

When high modes fail despite a capable HDMI input and correct dock/AC, check cable class. Prefer KEEP the included Ultra High Speed cable when the picture already meets your target. Shared bandwidth framing matches our console HDMI cable guide (class only—not a second Switch SKU list).

4. Third-party docks (honesty, not a list)

Verify with Nintendo / the maker

Third-party docks may advertise “Switch 2 ready.” Handshake and firmware behavior can be contested. We do not publish a third-party dock compatibility matrix or shopping roundup. If you consider one, verify with Nintendo guidance and the manufacturer—then prefer the official dock when TV Mode already works.

Practical checklist

  • Use Switch 2 dock + NGN-01-class AC + Ultra High Speed HDMI.
  • Select the correct TV HDMI input / game mode.
  • If picture already works → KEEP YOURS; stop.
  • Do not buy a USB-C video cable hoping to skip the dock for TV Mode.
  • Leave Engine Record / capture off for play-only.

FAQ

USB-C cable only to a monitor?

Do not assume Alt Mode success. Use the Switch 2 dock HDMI path per Nintendo.

What is required for TV Mode?

Switch 2 dock, Switch 2 AC (NGN-01), Ultra High Speed HDMI, and the console—per Nintendo Support.

Do I need capture to play on a TV?

No. Play-only is dock → HDMI → display.

Third-party docks?

Verify with Nintendo and the maker. No SKU matrix on this site.
